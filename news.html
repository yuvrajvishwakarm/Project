There are restaurants that open their doors, and then there are those that open their world. Scarlett House in Juhu belongs to the latter. It doesn’t shout for attention; it draws you in softly; with the glow of its textures, the ease of its atmosphere, and the way it makes you feel part of something familiar yet quietly extraordinary. Much of that feeling comes from the people behind it. Malaika Arora, known widely as a style icon, fitness enthusiast, and someone who has mastered the art of reinvention, brings more than her name to Scarlett House. She brings a philosophy. Her love for spaces that balance elegance with comfort, indulgence with mindfulness is evident in every detail; from the earthy tones to the little touches of glamour, and even in the thoughtful curation of the menu. Scarlett House isn’t just another business venture for her; it is a reflection of her lifestyle and values. Dhaval Udeshi, on the other hand, is a storyteller of hospitality. With a track record that spans beloved spaces like Gigi, Donna Deli, and Kaia Goa, he has built his reputation on creating restaurants that don’t just serve meals, but foster belonging. For him, it’s never about chasing trends or building the flashiest room in town. It’s about rhythm, understanding the pulse of a neighbourhood, the culture of a community, and creating spaces that feel like they’ve always been there. At Scarlett House, his instinct for intimacy and authenticity shapes everything from design choices to how guests experience the room. Together, they both have created something rare: a restaurant that doesn’t position itself as “the next big thing,” but as a place where people can feel at home, and yet, elevated. Scarlett House challenges conventional nightlife with its Hydration Bar, offers the comfort of neighbourhood dining with the edge of innovation, and carries the imprint of family, philosophy, and fearless creativity. In this conversation, Malaika Arora and Dhaval Udeshi open up about what Scarlett House means to them, the choices that shaped it, and how they see it growing while staying true to its soul. It’s less about business strategy, more about identity, and that’s exactly what makes Scarlett House worth listening to. Malaika, Scarlett House feels deeply personal, from its menu to its ambience. When you walk through the space, what moments or details make you think, “This is so me”? Scarlett House really is an extension of me. I’ve always gravitated toward spaces that feel warm, elegant, and effortless, and that’s exactly what I wanted people to experience here. From the earthy tones and textures to the little touches of glamour, it’s all very reflective of my personality. Even the menu has moments where I see myself: wholesome, balanced dishes with just the right indulgence. When I walk in, it feels like home, but also like the kind of place I’d want to dress up and go out to.
